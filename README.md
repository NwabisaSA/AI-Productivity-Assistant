# Sammy AI Assistant

SAMMY WELLNESS SPA — MANDATORY APPLICATION REQUIREMENTS

Build SAMMY Wellness Spa AI Virtual Assistant as a professional, modern and responsive AI-powered wellness spa productivity application.

The application must combine a wellness-spa business interface with an AI virtual assistant.

Use the brand colours:

White — primary background

Soft Pink — primary accent, buttons and highlights

Grey — secondary UI elements and professional contrast

The overall design must feel:

Elegant • Calm • Professional • Friendly • Modern • Premium

Because this project is being built with only 10 Lovable credits, prioritise the following requirements and do not waste development effort on unnecessary advanced features.

10. RESPONSIBLE AI DISCLAIMER — REQUIRED

This is a mandatory project requirement.

Display a visible disclaimer on AI-related pages.

Use:

Responsible AI Notice: AI-generated responses are provided for productivity and informational support. AI may produce inaccurate or incomplete information. Always review AI-generated content before using it or sending it to clients. SAMMY AI does not provide medical diagnosis or professional medical advice.

For wellness-related questions, the AI should encourage users to consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional when necessary.

The AI must:

Identify AI-generated information

Encourage human review

Avoid fabricated information

Avoid pretending to be a medical professional

Avoid inventing spa prices, appointments or policies

Avoid requesting unnecessary sensitive information

This is aligned with the project's requirement to identify AI limitations, risks, bias and validation steps.
